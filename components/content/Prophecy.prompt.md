**Prophecy** — the canon quote / scripture block: big italic Cormorant + mono attribution.

```jsx
<Prophecy cite="The Heartspire — Site of the First Collision">
  "They did not fall in love. Love fell out of them —
  and the universe caught it before it hit the ground."
</Prophecy>
```

- `variant`: `gold` (default) | `nebula` | `bright`.
- `rule`: hairline above the quote (default on). `align`: `center` (default) | `left`.
- Wrap actual quotation marks in the text; the brand quotes with literal `"…"`.
