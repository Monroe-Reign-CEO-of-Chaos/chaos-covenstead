**Checkbox** — a glass box that checks with a gold diamond (◆).

```jsx
<Checkbox label="I have read the Chaos Bible" defaultChecked />
<Checkbox label="Bind me to the Pulse" onChange={(on) => console.log(on)} />
```

- Controlled (`checked` + `onChange`) or uncontrolled (`defaultChecked`).
- `label`, `disabled`. The check glyph is the gold ◆ ritual mark.
