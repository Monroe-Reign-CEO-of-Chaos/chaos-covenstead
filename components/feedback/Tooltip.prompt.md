**Tooltip** — a mono-caps glass chip on hover/focus.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="Sealed in Echo Chamber I" placement="top">
  <Badge tone="nebula">Entombed</Badge>
</Tooltip>
```

- `label`, `placement` (`top` default | `bottom` | `left` | `right`).
- Wrap any focusable element; the tip is keyboard-accessible.
