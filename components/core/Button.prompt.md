**Button** — the Covenstead's action surface: beveled gold-glass on night, or the cold Dominion stamp.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="md" as="a" href="#">Step into the Pulse</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">Tour Dates</Button>
<Button variant="solid">Stream Now</Button>
<Button variant="dominion">Press Kit</Button>
```

- `variant`: `primary` (gold glass, default), `ghost` (nebula outline), `solid` (gold gradient, high emphasis), `dominion` (sterile black stamp — only inside `.theme-dominion`).
- `size`: `sm` | `md` | `lg`.
- Labels are mono + UPPERCASE by convention; keep them short and declarative.
