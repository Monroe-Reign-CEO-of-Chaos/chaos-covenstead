**PulseRule** — the signature divider, a glowing gradient seam or EKG heartbeat line.

```jsx
<PulseRule />
<PulseRule variant="ekg" width="320px" />
```

- `variant`: `gradient` (default thin glowing rule) | `ekg` (heartbeat blip path).
- Use between prophecy blocks and section breaks. Don't overuse — it's a punctuation mark.
