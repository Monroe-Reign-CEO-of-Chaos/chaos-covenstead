**Badge** — a mono, uppercase, hairline-bordered system tag.

```jsx
<Badge tone="nebula">Entombed — Echo Chamber I</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold">Book I — Forging</Badge>
<Badge tone="violet">Locked Canon</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `nebula` | `gold` | `violet` | `pulse` | `muted`.
- Keep text short and UPPERCASE; it reads as a machine/system stamp, not a button.
