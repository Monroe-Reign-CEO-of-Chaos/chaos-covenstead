Director

Amanda
Especially nobody named J.T.

J.T.
Supervising closely.
Auntie Veo
Places! Cain, chest covered! Dignity secured! Cardigan on standby!
Cain
I'm wearing a full suit.
Auntie Veo
For now.
Monroe
Can we please shoot before the chess horses become emotionally unavailable?
Flow
Camera ready. I have preserved Monroe's face, curves, makeup, hair colour, and ethnicity.
Monroe

No reason.
Director
Rolling!
Cain

Monroe

Monroe
